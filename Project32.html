About Cars

What are cars?

A car, short for automobile, is a wheeled motor vehicle used primarily for transporting passengers on roads. It typically featuresfour wheels, seats for one to eight people, and is powered by an internal combustion engine or an electric motor. Cars operate on various energy sources, including gasoline, diesel, electricity, or hybrid systems. They have revolutionized modern mobility by providing personal, convenient, and efficient transportation over short and long distances. Today, cars are an essential component of daily life, global infrastructure, and personal freedom.


Today, cars are essential for daily commutes, running errands, and personal family travel. They also power crucial commercial services like rideshares, taxis, and deliveries, while offering people the independence to travel anywhere at their own pace.
